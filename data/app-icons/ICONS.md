# Artwork

Bundled third-party application PNGs were removed in 0.15.0 because their individual artwork redistribution notices had not been verified. `sources.json` retains catalog identifiers and attribution data for matching; it does not grant rights. Missing icons fall back to the desktop theme or generic utility icons.

Live catalog artwork is fetched from configured Flathub catalogs; publisher terms continue to apply. Forge does not claim ownership of third-party artwork. Forge’s own icon and screenshots are covered by the project MIT license.
