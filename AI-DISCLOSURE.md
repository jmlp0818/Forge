# AI assistance disclosure

Forge was developed through extensive AI-assisted sessions. Substantial portions of the application Python code, helper scripts, tests, documentation, packaging, and project assets were generated or edited by AI. Exact per-line proportions and the origin of every previous asset have not been independently audited. No comprehensive human security review is claimed.

Version 0.15.0's packaging scripts and manifests are AI-assisted and intended for GitHub distribution. They are **not eligible as Flathub manifests under the policy checked on 2026-10-24**. A human considering Flathub would need to independently author eligible packaging and conduct the submission/review themselves. AI-generated application content must be disclosed as required by that service. Do not describe this project as human-written or independently security-audited.

Third-party app names, catalog metadata and artwork are external materials, not original Forge assets. Unverified bundled third-party PNGs were removed in 0.15.0.
